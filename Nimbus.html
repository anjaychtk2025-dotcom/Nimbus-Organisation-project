The Future is Frictionless

Welcome to the dawn of a new era in terrestrial transportation. The Nimbus Car is a Research & Development initiative designed to eliminate friction, reduce carbon footprints to zero, and redefine the very concept of the journey.

Hyper-Sus™ Technology

At the heart of the Nimbus lies our proprietary Hyper-Sus™ system. Utilizing onboard superconducting magnets, the vehicle levitates up to 10 centimeters above the road. This eliminates rolling resistance, allowing us to achieve speeds exceeding 400 km/h with a silence that is profound.

Intelligent Core

The Nimbus is equipped with the "Nimbus Core," a neural processing unit capable of 400 trillion operations per second. It communicates with city infrastructure in real-time via a 6G mesh network to create an accident-free traffic flow.

Sustainability

Powered by a solid-state hydrogen fuel cell with a range of 1,200 kilometers. The only emission is pure water vapor.
